Kindred Design System

Playful, colorful, still trustworthy.

The shared visual language for the Kindred patient site. Everything here is driven by design-system.css — change a token there and it updates everywhere.

Color

Four brand colors on a pale lavender-white. Plum leads; teal, coral, and sunny are accents.

Accessibility note: plum and ink both pass WCAG AA for text on the light background. Teal, coral, and sunny are bright — use them as fills, borders, or large shapes with dark ink text on top, not as small colored text on white.
